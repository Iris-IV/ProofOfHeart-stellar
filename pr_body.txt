## 📌 Description
This PR aligns the authorization conventions across revenue claiming functions by requiring the caller to authorize when claiming creator revenue. While funds were never at risk (since the payout destination was fixed to `campaign.creator`), `claim_creator_revenue` now enforces `campaign.creator.require_auth()` to ensure symmetry with `claim_revenue`, which already enforces contributor authorization.
